# JSM TRAVEL V99 — Hotel Gallery Additions

This Batch 2 revision includes 20 additional hotel photos supplied for the JSM TRAVEL project.

- 15 Ain Sokhna photos: `ain-sokhna-extra-014` through `030` (selected numbers)
- 5 Hurghada photos: `hurghada-extra-001` through `005`

These files are intentionally stored under `hotel-images/gallery-additions/` so they do not overwrite the existing hotel primary images.

Important: the supplied photos have not been assigned to individual hotel records here because the exact hotel-to-photo mapping was not supplied. They should not be presented as a specific hotel's gallery until that mapping is verified.
